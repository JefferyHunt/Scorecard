# Scorecard

A scorecard for board games and card games. Open it at
https://jefferyhunt.github.io/Scorecard/.

## Add it to your Home Screen

1. Open the link in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap Share, then tap Add to Home Screen.
3. Leave Open as Web App turned on, and tap Add.

## Files

- `index.html`: the app.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, and `icon-512.png`: the Home Screen name and icons.
- `sw.js`: offline support. After you change a file, the app shows the new version the second time it opens.
